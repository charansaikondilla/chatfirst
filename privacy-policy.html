Privacy Policy

App: chatfirst  ·  Last updated: September 27, 2026  ·  Effective: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how chatfirst ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you connect your Instagram account to our application through the Meta (Facebook) platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorise chatfirst, we may access the following information from your Instagram Business or Creator account through the official Instagram Graph API:

  • Your Instagram username, user ID, and profile name
  • Posts, reels, and stories you have published
  • Comments posted on your content by other users
  • Direct messages received in your professional inbox
  • Engagement metadata (likes count, comment count) on your posts

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or personal identification documents.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information accessed through the Instagram Graph API is used solely to provide the following features:

  • Automatically posting replies to comments on your Instagram posts
  • Sending automated responses to direct messages in your Instagram inbox
  • Displaying your media library so you can configure automation rules

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside of delivering the chatfirst service to you.

3. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens issued by Meta are stored securely and encrypted at rest. We do not permanently store the content of your comments or messages beyond what is operationally necessary to process and deliver automated replies. Temporary processing data is deleted within 24 hours.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to third parties. We do not share your Instagram data with advertisers, analytics companies, or any unrelated services. The only data transmission is between our servers and Meta's Graph API on your behalf.

5. Meta Platform Data Policy

Our use of information received from the Instagram Graph API is subject to Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies. We comply with all applicable Meta data policies.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may revoke chatfirst's access to your Instagram account at any time by visiting Instagram Settings → Apps and Websites → Active → chatfirst → Remove. Revoking access immediately stops all data collection.

To request deletion of all data we have stored about you, please visit our Data Deletion page or email us at the address below.

7. Cookies

chatfirst does not use tracking cookies. If session cookies are used, they exist solely to maintain your authenticated session and are not shared with third parties.

8. Children's Privacy

chatfirst is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of chatfirst after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
