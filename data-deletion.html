Data Deletion Request

chatfirst  ·  App ID: 1101338952421168

You have the right to request deletion of any personal data that chatfirst has stored in connection with your Instagram account. This page explains how to do that.

Option 1: Revoke Access Directly (Fastest)

The fastest way to stop all data collection and remove chatfirst's access to your account is to revoke the app in Instagram Settings:

  1. Open Instagram on your phone
  2. Go to your profile → tap the three lines (☰) → Settings and privacy
  3. Tap Apps and Websites
  4. Find chatfirst under Active apps
  5. Tap Remove

Revoking access immediately invalidates all access tokens. chatfirst can no longer read or write any data on your account from that moment.

Option 2: Request Full Data Deletion by Email

To request deletion of all stored data (access tokens, configuration, logs), email us with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your Instagram username so we can identify your account.

Send deletion request to:

aiverse59@gmail.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request
Include: Your Instagram username

What happens after your request:

We will acknowledge your request within 3 business days and complete all data deletion within 30 days. We will email you a confirmation once deletion is complete.

What Data We Delete

  • Your Instagram User ID and access tokens
  • Any automation rules or configuration you set up
  • Temporary processing logs associated with your account
